# Code of Conduct

Holt is built for people who run stores, and the people who use it, report
problems with it and work on it come from very different backgrounds. Everyone
who takes part in this project (in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other
project space) is expected to keep it a place where that is easy.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Be patient with people who are new to the project, to programming or to
  retail. Nobody knows all three.
- Give feedback that is specific and that the other person can act on, and take
  feedback the same way.
- Keep other people's private information private, including anything a store
  shares to show a problem.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks about who someone is: their gender,
  sexual orientation, race, ethnicity, religion, age, disability, appearance or
  level of experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging others to do any of the above.
- Publishing someone's private information without their clear permission.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion after being asked to stop.

## Reporting a problem

Tell the maintainer privately, at the email address in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), with "conduct" in the subject. Say what happened,
where, and who was involved; links help. Reports are read by the maintainer and
kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer looks into every report and decides what is fair: a private
word, a public correction, removing a comment, or a temporary or permanent ban
from the project's spaces. Someone who reports in good faith is never penalised
for doing so.

This applies in every project space, and anywhere someone is speaking for the
project.
