Find it under **Admin › Audit Trail**. It needs the **See the audit trail** permission, which owners and administrators start with.

## What it shows

One list, newest first, of the things you might need to look back on, with who did each and when:

- **Orders and payments:** refunds, voided payments, cancelled sales, deleted quotes and other changes to orders.
- **Prices:** product prices, vendor costs and imported price books.
- **People and access:** roles, staff, passwords set by an admin, PINs, and viewing the app as another role.
- **Settings:** store settings, integration keys, automations, email wording, payment types and tax.
- **Stock, customers, books and commission, and data:** counts cleared, merges, journal entries, commission payouts, deletes, exports and backups.

Where something still exists, its line links to it, such as the order.

## Finding something

- **Who:** part of their email address, in any case.
- **What:** one of the groups above.
- **From** and **To:** days in your store's time.

Nothing on the list can be changed or deleted.

## What it never holds

Passwords, PINs, the values of integration keys, and gift card barcodes. Settings are recorded by which field changed, not what it was set to.
